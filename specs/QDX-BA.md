# QDX-BA v0.1

Draft advanced block acceleration profile. QDX-BA extends QDX-B and remains optional. Filesystem correctness must not depend on it.

Capabilities: READ_OR, WRITE_OR, MULTI_WRITE, COPY, QUEUED_COMMANDS.

QDX-BA does not define mirror, RAID, virtual disks, checksums, repair, snapshots, or pools.
